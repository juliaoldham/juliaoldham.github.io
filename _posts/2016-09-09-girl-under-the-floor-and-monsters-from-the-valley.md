"Girl Under the Floor" (2016) is a short, musical animation about monster who is dancing to the beat of the knocking of a dead girl knocking on the floorboards. The video was made during a residency at Wassaic Project in Wassaic, NY, which is housed in an abandoned grain mill. The building has a very ghostly vibe, and inspired this playful short.

"Monsters From the Valley" is a related project. It is a comic book bestiary of imaginary monsters published in 2016. [It can be purchased here.](https://juliaoldham.bigcartel.com/product/monsters-from-the-valley-a-comic-book-pre-order) This project will be released as a hand-pulled, limited edition portfolio with DIY Printing in Cincinnati on Halloween, 2019.
