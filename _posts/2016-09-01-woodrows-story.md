_Woodrow's Story_ (2020 - ) is a graphic novel about my senior rescue pomeranian, Woodrow, and his recovery from horrific neglect and abuse. This project is currently in progress.

Read about _Woodrow's Story_ in ["Cabin Fever with Julia Oldham,"](https://www.arcadeprojectzine.com/features/cabin-fever-with-julia-oldham) by Roman Kalinovski at Arcade Project and ["Art on the Wild Side,"](https://eugeneweekly.com/2020/05/21/art-on-the-wild-side/) by Bob Keefer in Eugene Weekly.
