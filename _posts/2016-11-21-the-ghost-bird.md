Created during a 2016 residency in the Everglades National Park in South Florida with Artists in Residence in the Everglades, this hybrid video-animation, _The Ghost Bird_, follows birder/naturalist Kim Acker as she searches for the elusive ghost bird, the Ivory Billed Woodpecker.

The Ivory Billed Woodpecker was thought to be extinct until an alleged sighting in 2005 in Arkansas.
