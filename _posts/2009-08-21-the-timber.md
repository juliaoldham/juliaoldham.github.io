The Timber, a video series from 2008-2009, began with a residency in the Ferguson Timber in Marshalltown, Iowa. During my residency, I observed the behaviors of the insects and invertebrates surrounding me, and translated their activities into choreography that I performed in front of the camera. Commissioned by Art in General.
