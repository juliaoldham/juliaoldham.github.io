My video Dendrostalkers will be included in ["Trees, We Breathe,"](https://www.wavehill.org/calendar/trees-we-breathe) a group exhibition at Wave Hill in the Bronx from May 31 - September 1, 2025.

![](assets/img/news/436467528_425415640352331_3605572699728610769_n-1024x538.jpg)
May, 2024: The L.L. Stewart Fellowship through the Patricia Valian Reser Center for the Creative Arts, Corvallis, OR will allow me to collaborate with Dr. Christopher Still, a researcher in forestry at Oregon State University this coming year. Stay tuned for more info about this project!

![](assets/img/news/fluxnet-announce-1024x851.jpg)
This year I am participating in fluxART, a pilot science and art residency program with FLUXNET, with scientist Christopher Still.

![](assets/img/news/sitka.jpg)
November - December, 2024: I'll be working with master printer Mika Aono at [Sitka Center for Art and Ecology](https://www.sitkacenter.org/)

**Recent Exhibitions**:

I had both video and print work included in _A Future Eco_ at [Arsenal Gallery](https://www.nycgovparks.org/art-and-antiquities/arsenal-gallery) in New York City in September, 2024. We are planning a collaborative publication about this show.

I had work included in [_Forest Bathing_](https://elycenter.org/forest-bathing), a group show at [Ely Center for Contemporary Art](https://elycenter.org/) in New Haven, CT in September, 2024.
